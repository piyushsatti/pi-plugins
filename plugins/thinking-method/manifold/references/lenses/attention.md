# Attention and upkeep

Optional plugin-authored attention guide, not a standard provision or domain policy.
Apply with the selected skill and [shared method](../guide.md).

## When useful

The model, review or routine risks consuming more human attention than its purpose justifies.

## Additional questions

- What is the smallest view that preserves distinctions that change the decision?
- What must stay current, who maintains it, and how would stale links be noticed?
- Can shared source context avoid repeated intake and duplicated records?

## Failure modes

Compulsory exhaustive interviews, stale summaries, unnecessary duplicated state, and concise prose that drops a meaning-changing condition.

## Evidence expectations

Existing workload constraints, observed maintenance experience when supplied, explicit ownership and revision basis. Predicted effort savings stay predictions.

## Limits and conflicts

Cannot invent an effort target, remove mandatory local limits, or infer empirical usability from shorter text or a screenshot. If another lens suggests a competing action, record the affected needs,
criteria and consequences as a local choice. Do not silently rank lenses or launch
another agent. Any suggested criterion must be labeled a proposal with a reason.
