# Consequences and recovery

Optional plugin-authored attention guide, not a standard provision or domain policy.
Apply with the selected skill and [shared method](../guide.md).

## When useful

Error costs, reversibility or a proposed commitment make uncertainty consequential.

## Additional questions

- Which exact requirement or claim carries the tolerance, and who set it?
- What could fail at the chosen boundary, and who bears the consequences?
- What can recovery change now, and which historical outcome remains unchanged?

## Failure modes

Universal strictness grades, unexamined conditions silently excluded, acceptance erasing an unmet result, and recovery rewriting a missed deadline.

## Evidence expectations

Requirement-specific limits, supplied evidence for the relevant conditions, consequence reasoning and any scoped acceptance or recovery decision.

## Limits and conflicts

Cannot set domain thresholds or certify safety, legal compliance or operational effectiveness. Missing evidence blocks only the dependent conclusion. If another lens suggests a competing action, record the affected needs,
criteria and consequences as a local choice. Do not silently rank lenses or launch
another agent. Any suggested criterion must be labeled a proposal with a reason.
