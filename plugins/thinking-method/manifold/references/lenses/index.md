# Optional lenses

Select by circumstances and the pending decision, not industry. These five guides
add questions without changing meanings or assigning authority. A lens does not
launch an agent. Surface competing lens recommendations as a local choice.

- [Stakeholders and trade-offs](stakeholders.md): Several accounts, missing perspectives, concentrated benefits or distributed costs affect the decision.
- [Operations and coordination](operations.md): Recurring work, handoffs, dependencies, time windows or exception handling matter.
- [Interfaces and automation](interfaces.md): A programmatic consumer, integration, structured output or automation choice constrains the case.
- [Consequences and recovery](consequences.md): Error costs, reversibility or a proposed commitment make uncertainty consequential.
- [Attention and upkeep](attention.md): The model, review or routine risks consuming more human attention than its purpose justifies.

Ontology, taxonomy, mereology, behavior, history and assessment are complementary
modeling views of the same case; they are not competing lenses.
