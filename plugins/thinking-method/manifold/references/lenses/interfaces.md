# Interfaces and automation

Optional plugin-authored attention guide, not a standard provision or domain policy.
Apply with the selected skill and [shared method](../guide.md).

## When useful

A programmatic consumer, integration, structured output or automation choice constrains the case.

## Additional questions

- Why is the function wanted, and which consumer/version is actually chosen?
- What input/output contract, identifiers and error meanings does that interface require?
- What is absent, unknown or incompatible, and what may a structural check actually conclude?

## Failure modes

Circular justification, schema compliance mistaken for semantic adequacy, an invented consumer, and treating a record as the observation act.

## Evidence expectations

The actual selected interface contract and its revision, examples of supported inputs/results and stated checking limits. A hypothetical validator supplies no operating evidence.

## Limits and conflicts

Cannot invent a schema, registry mapping or service merely to package text. Program and agent labels do not establish capability or authority. If another lens suggests a competing action, record the affected needs,
criteria and consequences as a local choice. Do not silently rank lenses or launch
another agent. Any suggested criterion must be labeled a proposal with a reason.
