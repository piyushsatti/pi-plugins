# Operations and coordination

Optional plugin-authored attention guide, not a standard provision or domain policy.
Apply with the selected skill and [shared method](../guide.md).

## When useful

Recurring work, handoffs, dependencies, time windows or exception handling matter.

## Additional questions

- What triggers work and makes each input ready?
- Who produces and receives each handoff; what makes it usable?
- What persists after this occurrence ends, and what happens on a missing or failed input?

## Failure modes

A completed subprocess mistaken for final outcome, unowned exceptions, dropped ongoing responsibility and successful components with a failed combined handoff.

## Evidence expectations

Applicable process/configuration, actual assignments and authority basis, relevant handoff records and timings. Distinguish intended behavior from supplied execution records.

## Limits and conflicts

Cannot infer sustained reliability from one run or prescribe a universal state machine or response handler. If another lens suggests a competing action, record the affected needs,
criteria and consequences as a local choice. Do not silently rank lenses or launch
another agent. Any suggested criterion must be labeled a proposal with a reason.
