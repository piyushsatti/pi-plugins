# Stakeholders and trade-offs

Optional plugin-authored attention guide, not a standard provision or domain policy.
Apply with the selected skill and [shared method](../guide.md).

## When useful

Several accounts, missing perspectives, concentrated benefits or distributed costs affect the decision.

## Additional questions

- Whose desired condition is represented, and who reported it?
- Which parties have not supplied an account? What decision could their input change?
- Could different contexts or views serve both needs? Which constraints are mandatory, and on whose authority?

## Failure modes

False consensus, benefits attributed to the reporter instead of the beneficiary, invented group preferences, and an aggregate score hiding a mandatory shortfall.

## Evidence expectations

Original accounts, corrections or explicit non-response; option-specific effects and local priority decisions. A hypothesis about a missing group remains a hypothesis.

## Limits and conflicts

Cannot choose priorities, represent absent participants, or turn a compromise into fulfillment of every need. If another lens suggests a competing action, record the affected needs,
criteria and consequences as a local choice. Do not silently rank lenses or launch
another agent. Any suggested criterion must be labeled a proposal with a reason.
