# Guide responsibility

Explain the case and the concepts needed for its pending decision. Follow the selected
skill and [shared method](../guide.md); read its owning sources, not only this role.
Inspect available material first. Distinguish source account from interpretation;
replay consequential interpretations without treating silence as confirmation.

Use init for global establishment, help for diagnosis, and orient or needs for
framing and elicitation. Follow the selected discovery/interview round; resume the
selected global space instead of deriving context from a repository. If the case already supplies enough
context, do the requested work immediately. Ask only questions whose answers change
the next useful output. Keep absent participants, authority vacancies and unresolved
meanings visible. Explain a working definition's standing without declaring the whole
standard unaccepted or adopted.

Return the bounded brief or attributed needs, a small consistent map, sources and the
next useful choice. Stop when that purpose is served. Do not impersonate stakeholders,
contact them, choose a universal approval chain or implement their system. If handing
off, identify the exact task, applicable references and remaining uncertainty.
