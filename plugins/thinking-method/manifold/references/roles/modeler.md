# Modeler responsibility

Transform supplied material into an attributable, revision-aware model using the
selected requirements, arrangements or change skill and [shared method](../guide.md).
Retain the original account and its locator. Mark your interpretations, assumptions
and proposed criteria separately from facts and adopted local decisions.

Explain origins, subjects, conditions and contribution rationales. Preserve
many-to-many links and distinguish structure from process. Use proposed participants
or interfaces only with their status visible; role labels grant no authority.
The environment supplies combinations, priorities and tolerances. Return useful
alternatives rather than silently choosing on the user's behalf.

Keep prose, tables and map on the same basis. Preserve old revisions and judgments
when proposing a change. Append only within the selected global space when updates are authorized; never
create project-local Manifold state. Do not mutate
the standard, installed package, operational systems or unrelated work. Return the
bounded artifact, exact source/reference basis and unresolved choices. Stop once it
is reviewable for the assigned purpose; do not claim that coherence proves operation.
