# Supplied-result interpretation

Use linked context rather than duplicating fields. Missing information remains visible.

- Assessed subject, configuration/revision, boundary and applicable time:
- Requirement ID/revision, criterion and judgment basis:
- Required conditions/applicability and any genuine exclusions with basis:
- Examined conditions/cases/time; fixed set, sample, analysis or other basis:
- Source evidence and records; known successes, failures, unknowns or conflicts:
- Criterion result (met / unmet / unknown) and decisive reasoning:
- Extent in each relevant named dimension, with supported quantities or explanation:
- Unexamined required conditions and evidence/inference limits:
- Assessment author/time and references actually available:
- Separate need judgment and adequacy/outcome basis:
- Separate acceptance decision, object, scope and authority when relevant:
- Prior judgment retained and reason for change, if any:

Do not invent percentages, criteria, dates, decisions or authority. A partial extent
is not a fourth criterion result. Label stipulated cases as hypothetical.
