# Bounded Manifold handoff

The receiving contributor should be able to continue without earlier chat history.
Do not copy an entire corpus; supply exact, authorized locators or relevant excerpts.

- Task, selected capability and role; expected output and stopping condition:
- Global space and checkpoint, case identity/revision, purpose and environment brief:
- Applicable plugin/reference snapshot: source-manifest hash and specific owning
  files/provisions, standing and any local extension:
- Available source locators/revisions, original accounts and attribution:
- Current model and relationship/map basis:
- Assumptions, interpretations, confirmed facts and proposals distinguished:
- Prior assessments/decisions with criterion, conditions, evidence and scope:
- Changes since that basis and preserved prior conclusions:
- Unresolved choices, missing perspectives/references, affected conclusions and
  known recipient/authority or vacancy:
- Authorized actions and selected global space destination (or return in chat):
- Current discovery/interview mode and one next step; do not restart establishment:
- Verification already performed and its limits:

On receipt: verify these references and revisions are actually accessible, inspect
the supplied material and confirm the task boundary. Report exact missing items or
conflicts; continue unaffected work. Do not infer unavailable content, approvals or
operating evidence. Ask only for context that changes the next useful output.
