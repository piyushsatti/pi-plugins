# Space checkpoint

Use these prompts selectively for a useful, consolidated current view. Save only in
the selected global space through the space helper. A partial foundation is useful;
these are not required fields or a completeness/adoption checklist.

- Space display name, purpose and boundary; prior checkpoint if any:
- Current mode and the question this round addresses:
- Authorized document scope; source locators/revisions, attribution and access limits:
- Interview account in the speaker's terms, separate from agent interpretation:
- Current needs, requirements, systems and justified connections; a small matching map:
- Adopted criteria/decisions and their actual authority, distinct from proposals:
- Corrections since the prior basis, preserving earlier judgments and why they changed:
- Consequential gaps, absent perspectives and unexamined conditions:
- What the user chose to do next, or one recommended next step awaiting their choice:
- Applicable Manifold reference basis and standing; verification performed and limits:

At resume, verify the sources needed for the chosen next step. Do not redo the opening
or infer that a saved draft is accepted. The latest checkpoint helps find the current
view; earlier checkpoints remain available for provenance and disagreement.
